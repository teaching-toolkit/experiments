🎯 Spot the Bait

A YouTube savvy quiz — learn to tell the real from the clickbait

Before you start…

You're about to see 50 YouTube video previews — the thumbnail image, the title, the channel name, and the view count. For each one, you'll rate how trustworthy it looks using a slider.

Some videos are obvious clickbait designed to scare, trick, or mislead you. Some are more subtle. And some are perfectly fine videos that might look suspicious at first glance. After each rating, you'll get instant feedback and a short explanation.

What you'll learn to spot:

🚨 Fear-bait 💰 Financial scams 🤖 Impersonation channels 🎁 Fake giveaways 💊 Miracle cure claims 😡 Rage-bait 🔮 Conspiracy hooks ⏰ Fake urgency ✅ Genuine content

There are no trick questions — the answers are always explained. Take your time, trust your instincts, and don't worry about getting things wrong. That's how you learn.
